Guide — in our own words

  1. Chapter 1: after the mission “Down to Earth”, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning and follow her to meditate on the balcony (choose “Accept”). Source: PowerPyx
  2. Save manually BEFORE “Down to Earth” as insurance — this moment only exists in Chapter 1. Source: PowerPyx
  3. Meditate at all 18 spots; meditating as Jason AND Lucia at the same spot counts +2. Source: PowerPyx

Track at Progress > Collections > Exploration > Meditations. The balcony meditation raises the counter +1 but is not marked as a Meditation Spot — it is not confirmed whether it is required; PowerPyx earned the trophy after doing it, so just do it.

WARNING: the only potentially missable trophy. No mission replay, no New Game+ — a manual save is the only safety net.

Roadmap to Platinum — 7 stages

Built from the official descriptions: story chapters and missions, collectibles and counts, in order. Total estimate ~200–300 hours (PowerPyx ~200h; PushSquare ~300h). Story alone 60–80 hours.

  1. Stage 1 — Story, Chapters 1–2 + Chapter 1 meditation. Just Like Paradise · Paradise Lost · Trust · T**s Ass Drug Money · Positive Mental Attitude (do the balcony meditation — the only missable moment). ~15h.
  2. Stage 2 — Story, Chapters 3–5 to credits. Vice City, Baby! · Top Of The World, Ma · Break the Bank · Family Matters · DWNFALL · Hi-Jacked · Snow Storm · You Gotta Know When to Get Off. Key Cloner + Dye Pack Jammer unlock in Chapter 3. Save manually at the start and end of each chapter. ~45–65h.
  3. Stage 3 — Strangers & Freaks, 7 questlines. Road to Rehabilitation (Andy) · This Was No Boat Accident (Kenton) · The Lone Rider (Billy) · Strangers & Freaks (Kirk, Kyle, Yago, Pilot Pete) · Supply & Demand (Randy) · Spin On This! (Tyrone) · Fair Game (Bret). Note: Andy/Billy/Tyrone questlines do NOT count toward Strangers & Freaks. ~10–15h.
  4. Stage 4 — Partner crimes & combat. Hot Together · Retail Therapy · Gunslinger · Sneakthief · A Classic Blitz Play (safest via the Chapter 4 bank-vault mission — robbing a truck directly can bug it, damnmodz) · Maximum Withdrawal · Bad Luck, I Guess · The Truth Is Out There · Ten Pin (UNKNOWN) · Catch Me If You Can · Challenging Tank. ~8–12h.
  5. Stage 5 — Activities, races & skills. Master of None · The Shootist (42 golds — hardest after A Long Road; raise Focus first) · Top Fun · Earn Cash, Not Kudos · Baited My Tackle · Stick Fiddler · Personal Best (max Strength with Jason AND Lucia together) · Actions Speak Louder (500 Criminal Profile actions — avoid permanent Irredeemable). ~15–20h.
  6. Stage 6 — Collections, scanning & exploration. Cunning Stunts (250/397) · One Last Thing (50/80) · You WAiNKer! (100) · Don't Touch My Deck (3/18 sets) · Whip It Good (100 vehicles) · The Goodtime State · Pest Control (1 op per gang) · In The Beginning… (Apple Martini $10, Ocean View Rooftop Bar) · Capital Offense (blue Banshee through the showroom window) · Let’s Go Bowling. ~20–30h.
  7. Stage 7 — Robberies, fencing & 100% cleanup → Platinum. Dime on the Dollar ($250,000 marked bills) · Treat Yo Self ($100,000 spent) · Little Boxes (8 garages, $160,000) · On the Spot (10 vehicles) · A Long Road (100% Completion) · Vice Got a Price! Fastest legit money: 8 large bank vaults, up to $205,000 each.

Glitch & Farming

Apollo Aggregates Red Warehouse — money glitch

STATUS: DISPUTED — reported, not fully verified

Red warehouse (Apollo Aggregates, east of Hamlet, South Vice-Dale County, north of Key Lento). Loot the back-office cocaine + safe, fill two stash bags, store BOTH bags in your personal vehicle's trunk, save manually, reload — the warehouse loot respawns while the trunk contents stay. Repeat, then sell at Brian's Lockup, south-west Key Lento (keypad code 7700). About $8,640 per loop; first loop ~$11,000–$11,280.

For: KeeNgamer, allblogthings (20 Nov — reported working at launch). Against: afleurdenet (“No working infinite-money glitch exists right now”), vanotherlevel (18 Nov — no outlet had reported a working money glitch). Can be patched at any time.

Never leave bags on the ground or carry them during reload — they can vanish. And NEVER enter PSN/Xbox/Rockstar credentials on “cash generator” sites — those are phishing. Legit alternative: large bank vaults, up to $205,000 per bank (8 vaults).

Legal farming tricks (not glitches)

  • Focus farm: loop the 5-second shooting-range drill to raise Focus (Gunslinger + The Shootist). Source: vanotherlevel
  • Train tunnel escape: rail tunnel north of Ambrosia — police struggle on the rails, helicopters can't see you. Best for Hot Together and 4-star escapes. Source: PowerPyx
  • Double meditation: meditate as Jason AND Lucia at the same spot = +2. Source: PowerPyx
  • Double workout: work out with your partner — both gain Strength progress. Source: PowerPyx
  • 50% shotgun discount: clerk Andrew gives Jason (ex-military) 50% off the Fortune & Son Moreland 850 ($350 instead of $700) in the Chapter 1 weapons mission — and the discount sticks if you come back later. Source: vanotherlevel
  • Pay 'n' Spray only cleans the CAR — not your clothes or face. Losing stars is not the same as losing the description. Change clothes/hair to change the description police see. Source: vanotherlevel
  • Adrenaline extends Combat Focus (hold L1 > Triangle) — use it before Gunslinger attempts. Source: PowerPyx

Launch status — day-one patch

  • Pre-release updates v01.002.000 (13 Nov, ~1.07 GB PS5 / 2.18 GB Xbox) and v01.002.001 (16 Nov, ~602.5 MB PS5 / 1.6 GB Xbox) — Rockstar published NO patch notes. Both must be installed before playing. Source: dlcompare (IGN, VICE)
  • PSN outage 18 Nov evening (hours before unlock): login failures, ~10,000 reports (US) + ~6,000 (UK). Fixed by Wednesday midday. Sony's side, not the game. Source: Guardian, Cybernews, Axios
  • 0 glitched trophies (PowerPyx). No verified in-game bug list from any outlet as of 20 Nov — the game is rated very polished (Metacritic 99). Source: PowerPyx, cryptogames
  • Rumor “Rockstar apologised + fix 25 Nov” — UNVERIFIED, no support on any official Rockstar channel. Source: iGameMag
  • Frame rate: 30 FPS target, drops in dense areas; no 60 FPS mode. Source: VGC, PlayStation LifeStyle

Warnings — read before you play

  • Cheats disable trophies AND saving. Never use cheats on your platinum save.
  • Criminal Profile “Irredeemable” is PERMANENT — police become more aggressive (shoot on sight). Other profiles (Professional / Hardened / Violent / Psycho) are not permanent.
  • No mission replay, no New Game+. Manual saves are the only safety net: 15 manual slots + 1 autosave.
  • Rotate save slots during free-roam cleanup — don't overwrite your chapter saves (save-slot trap).
  • Prologue bugs reported: crashes / lost progress despite autosave, falling through the map after Lucia is tasered, one early Lucia mission softlock. Mitigation: save manually often, rotate slots.

Skunk Ape / Bigfoot (open-world)

EASTER EGG · CONFIRMED · PowerPyx, Kotaku, GameSpot, OutputLag, Game8

Creature crouched eating a deer carcass on the road south of Lake Cottonmouth, Grassrivers. Approach — it flees into the bushes; follow to a wooden effigy in a tree (green fog) — your character faints, a cutscene of furry feet approaches, you wake at the lake edge. Available from Chapter 1.

UFO abduction (The Truth Is Out There)

EASTER EGG · CONFIRMED · PowerPyx, GameSpot, OutputLag, GTAVibes

After Chapter 1, ALONE, road north-west of Starlet Motel, Kelly County, after 02:00 in-game. Find a parked car with flashing lights, follow the trail into the woods toward blue light — a UFO is lifting a woman. Help her, return to the car — the UFO returns and abducts you. Also a trophy.

Cameo Niko Bellic (“The Hitman”)

EASTER EGG · CONFIRMED · Kotaku, Beebom, Game8

Chapter 5 “Top of the World, Ma”, story mission “Taking Liberties”. Niko — older, grey-haired, voiced by Michael Hollick — appears in Giorgio's penthouse in 2 cutscenes. Credited as “The Hitman”. Giorgio shouts “Daddy's back, bitches!” (GTA IV's opening line).

Mandy — Key Lento (mystery)

ODDITY · CONFIRMED clues · Gamebrott, Tech2Geek, FandomWire, OutputLag

COMMUNITY THEORY (NOT fact): “Mandy is a psychopath/serial killer.” No confirmation from any cutscene or Rockstar — always present it as theory.
